Longitudinal Outcome Tracking

One record, from enrolment to employment.

Government training programmes track attendance and certificates well — but lose the trail the moment training ends. This prototype gives every candidate a single Golden ID that follows them through course completion and into their employment outcome, so training centers and program administrators can finally see what happened after the course.
