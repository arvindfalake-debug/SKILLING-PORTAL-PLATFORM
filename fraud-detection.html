Fraud & duplicate-registration watch

Ghost enrolments are a common way training funds get siphoned — many "different" candidates registered from the same phone number or the same device/IP look identical on paper but not underneath. This automatically clusters candidates who share a phone number or an IP address so an admin can investigate before certifying or releasing funds for them.

Demo only — a phone number is illustrative here, and in production this would also weigh Aadhaar-linked device signals, registration timing patterns and biometric attendance mismatches, not phone/IP alone.
