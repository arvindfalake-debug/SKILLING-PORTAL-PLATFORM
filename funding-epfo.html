Funding & EPFO bridge

Instead of paying training centers on the strength of a self-submitted placement report, the idea: hold each candidate's funding milestone in escrow, and release it only once that candidate's employment shows up independently — for example a new EPF (provident fund) contribution from a verified employer. This page simulates that check and the resulting fund release; it does not call a real EPFO/UAN system.

Demo only — amounts, escrow rules and the "EPFO check" below are illustrative placeholders, not a real EPFO/UAN integration or an actual escrow account.
